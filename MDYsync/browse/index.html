Guide

How to use Interactive Daf

  1. Pick a tractate, daf, and amud above, or use the "Previous page"/"Next page" buttons to flip through in order.
  2. Tap any word on the page to play the shiur right here, starting at the exact moment it's discussed.
  3. Switch English/Hebrew and Regular/Chazarah Daf above to browse whichever recording you want a tapped word to jump to.

Tapping a word only works for a daf that already has a synced shiur -- others will show what's available once it does.
