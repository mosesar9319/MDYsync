Prototype guide

How to use DafSync

  1. Choose a lecture video (local file, direct link, or YouTube) and enter a daf reference, then press "Load daf."
  2. Use "Sync from video" to generate real timestamps automatically from the video's on-screen caption box, via the desktop app or a Google Drive link.
  3. Click a phrase on the daf to jump the video to that point. During playback, the daf auto-scrolls to follow along.
  4. To align by hand instead, open "Edit synchronization," select a phrase, and press M while listening to mark its start time and advance to the next.
  5. Export the result as a reusable alignment JSON, or import one with "Import sync."

YouTube playback is controlled through YouTube's embedded player.
