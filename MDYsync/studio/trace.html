How this works

Drag a box around the Gemara column, then Detect lines. Each line's own left/right edge and word gaps are found by ink density, not text recognition -- drag a line's edges to correct it, or use the tools that appear beside it to split or merge. Lines flagged amber usually mean a printed abbreviation Sefaria's raw text spells out in full; click one to mark it.
