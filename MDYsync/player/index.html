Guide

How to use the player

  1. Pick a tractate, daf, and amud above, or arrive here already loaded from a link on the library page.
  2. Click a phrase on the daf to jump the video to that point. During playback, the daf auto-scrolls to follow along.
  3. Switch the daf view to "Vilna page" for a real page image instead of plain text, or overlay it directly on the video.
  4. Sign in to save favorites and resume a shiur where you left off.

YouTube playback is controlled through YouTube's embedded player.
